1. Who we are

This service is operated by Sakuraki Co., Ltd. (櫻起株式会社), the operator of IKINUKI - 雅 -, a private head spa located at Dear Heim Asakusa 2F, 3-16-1 Asakusa, Taito-ku, Tokyo 111-0032, Japan. Contact: info@ikinuki-miyabi.jp

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to our website (ikinuki-miyabi.jp), our pre-visit counseling form, and our Instagram direct-message assistant ("IKINUKI DM Assistant"), which replies to messages sent to our Instagram account through the official Instagram API provided by Meta Platforms, Inc.

3. Information we collect

  • Through Instagram messages: your Instagram user ID and username, the text of messages you send us, and the time they were sent.
  • Through reservations: name, email address, phone number, preferred date and time, and optional notes (for example an anniversary date). Payment details are processed by Square and are never stored by us.
  • Through the pre-visit counseling form: your name, booking date and time, number of guests, preferred language, hair and scalp condition, health conditions that may affect your treatment, allergies and ingredients to avoid, areas you prefer not to be touched, optional photos of your hairstyle, and optional survey answers (age group, country or region, how you found us, travel details).
  • Through the website: standard usage data collected by Meta Pixel for advertising measurement. See Meta's data policy for details.

4. How we use it

  • To answer your questions and guide you to our reservation page.
  • To manage reservations, send confirmations and reminders, and process cancellations and refunds under our cancellation policy.
  • To provide a safe treatment: to adjust the treatment, choose suitable products, and contact you in advance if needed, based on your counseling form answers.
  • To improve the accuracy of our automated replies and our services.

5. Automated replies and AI processing

Replies in Instagram direct messages may be generated automatically. To generate a reply, the text of your message may be transmitted to third-party AI service providers (Google LLC and/or Anthropic PBC) acting as our processors. We do not permit these providers to use your messages to train their models. Questions that require human judgment are forwarded to our staff. Counseling form answers are not sent to AI service providers.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf (Meta Platforms for messaging delivery, Google Cloud for hosting, the AI providers above, and Square for reservations and payment), and where required by law.

7. Retention

Instagram message data is retained for up to 90 days after the last message, then deleted. Counseling form answers (including hairstyle photos) are retained for 3 years from the date of your visit, then deleted, and can be viewed only by IKINUKI salon staff. Reservation records are retained for the period required by Japanese commercial and tax law.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. See our Data Deletion Instructions, or email info@ikinuki-miyabi.jp.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available at this URL.

3. 取得する情報

  • Instagramのメッセージを通じて：InstagramのユーザーID・ユーザー名、お送りいただいたメッセージの本文、送信日時。
  • ご予約を通じて：お名前、メールアドレス、電話番号、ご希望日時、任意の備考（記念日など）。決済情報はSquareが処理し、当店では保存しません。

4. 利用目的

  • ご質問への回答と、予約ページへのご案内。

5. 自動返信とAIによる処理

Instagramのダイレクトメッセージへの返信は自動生成される場合があります。返信を生成するため、メッセージ本文が当店の委託先であるAIサービス提供者（Google LLC および／または Anthropic PBC）に送信されることがあります。当店はこれらの提供者に対し、お客様のメッセージをAIモデルの学習に用いることを許可しません。判断を要するご質問はスタッフに引き継ぎます。カウンセリングフォームのご回答をAIサービス提供者に送信することはありません。

6. 第三者提供

個人情報を販売することはありません。当店に代わって処理を行う委託先（メッセージ配信のMeta Platforms、ホスティングのGoogle Cloud、上記AI提供者、予約・決済のSquare）および法令に基づく場合を除き、第三者に提供しません。

7. 保存期間

Instagramのメッセージデータは最終メッセージから最長90日間保存した後、削除します。カウンセリングフォームのご回答（髪型の写真を含む）はご来店日から3年間保存した後に削除し、閲覧できるのは当サロンのスタッフのみです。予約記録は商法・税法上必要な期間保存します。
