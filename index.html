CONCEPT

A quiet ritual, behind Senso-ji

IKINUKI is a private, reservation-only head spa with just two seats, hidden behind Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa. We hold one quiet hour entirely for you — a slow, deliberate ritual that returns you to a state of deep breath.

PHOTO 02｜サンプル画像実際に撮りたい：頭皮に触れる手元アップ（縦）。顔は写さず手と髪だけ

Two seats only. No waiting room, no phone ringing, no hurry. The space is designed around a single idea — that stillness itself is the greatest luxury we can offer.

Each ritual is performed unhurried, with full attention to the scalp, the breath, and the silence between them.
